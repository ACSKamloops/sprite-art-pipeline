# Preserve drawing while refining coordinated motion

Use this route when a strong generated key bank already exists but a face,
accessory or a few transitions fail. Freeze the actual best reference clip,
including its original timing; a weaker previous test is not the quality floor.
Score its drawing without deducting for an opaque matte.

Assign visible ownership before splitting paint. A head track needs source
head/neck pixels, attachment anchors and a protected collar/body. A rear scarf
track needs its visible tail mask, source-color witnesses, protected bib and
body occlusion. Removing/reassembling the original owners must reproduce every
key exactly. This recovers visible paint only, not hidden cloth or anatomy.
Review a translated head's neck join: even a one-pixel move can reveal an air
row. A deliberate source-colored cap is new authored underpaint, with a journal
and visual tradeoff, not an exact translation of the whole head/neck volume.

For a mixed-rate experiment, retain original key onsets and loop period. Union
the component clocks using `union_component_clocks.py`; keep rational holds in
APNG/manifest and the editor sidecar. Compare against unchanged drawings repeated
in those same slots. A 16-slot track with only five new cloth rasters has five
new cloth states, not sixteen new full-body poses. Source-key preservation and
editor roundtrips establish transport only.

Inspect actual corresponding sole/toe/ankle paint at the preceding key,
intermediate and following key. More boot separation is not necessarily better:
passing feet can occlude naturally, and a foot that reaches the next key too
early can produce a catch. Named IK controls, alpha centroids and equal part
areas do not prove the visible support-foot path. Record uncertainty when
occlusion prevents material correspondence. Check pelvis, torso, arm and head
coordination before expanding a leg-only rig; a protected upper body can itself
be the remaining motion limitation.

Distal rotation with nearest sampling preserves source colors, not every donor
texel, area or shape. Report repeats/omissions, actual silhouette and cuff join.
Literal cluster painting must retain its mask, expected source/cel hash, actual
changed pixels and replay. Rebase all ownership when upstream paint or grid
processing changes; old source maps do not automatically fit a new raster.

The 2026-10-20/21 generic-hero trials added an ImageGen scarf, a sharper owned
head, richer body colors, two lower-body bridges and five cloth midpoints. Cold
reviews disagreed on the new drawing: some found it comparable at8/10, later
reviews preferred the reference and rated candidates around7–7.5. The crowded
boot transitions and held upper body did not establish a motion win. A fixed
head improved local identity while newly painted bridge torsos still changed
volume. These findings are not9/10 acceptance or a new library-wide approval.
Normal-speed human judgment remains separate from exhaustive ordered stills.
