# Source-conditioned model animation: bounded methods and failure evidence

This is a diagnostic route, not an accepted original-character production pipeline. The Captain full-character source-hinge v4 was independently rejected at craft6/10 and motion5.5/10. Earlier passing geometry and contact checks did not approve its rendered art. Use the sequence below to locate errors, not to promise automatic high quality from a flat image.

## Freeze design and infer missing volume honestly

Keep an approved static source, native scale and hand/equipment contract. A portrait is neither a neutral model nor hidden anatomy. Author a separate body-only neutral view and label inferred side/back surfaces. Keep generated fused hero statues as failed controls if equipment, cloth and limbs cannot articulate independently. Save model source code/weight pins, licenses, exact inputs/outputs, runtime environment and resource bounds. Publicly accessible model weights do not imply every dependency is ungated.

Inspect neutral geometry from front, side, rear and quarter before assigning joints. Register hip, knee and ankle to actual visible plate/crease regions. In the local case the initial knee was halfway down the shin cuff: fixed model-informed rest placement was necessary before testing weights. These landmarks are authored estimates, not recovered anatomy.

## Paint once; animate the actual surface

Prefer fixed UV paint, source-attached materials and separate real equipment modules over unrelated per-frame redraws. Record original texture/UV/geometry signatures and paint ownership masks. Retain exact original texels outside the owned edit. A visible front projection supplies no true side/back paint. Reject UV overlap or fallback that invents detail as recovered source.

Source-conditioned AI can propose one static ornament texture, then a declared fixed registration can attach it to real geometry. Check alpha/glow and motif distortion; a generated front is still authored. In the shield experiment a transparency follow-up retained external haze. Sampling inside the shield through one fixed UV registration avoided transferring that haze onto a translucent physical panel, but did not fix the original rounded-panel silhouette. No pose-specific UV fitting was used.

## Validate motion independently of appearance

Freeze actual capture frame order, axes/units, source clock and retarget basis. Preserve raw input and declare cycle conditioning. The local CMU cycle has130source120Hz intervals,65exported60Hz poses and duration65/60seconds. Do not round each pose to17ms. Retargeted armour carry, contact phases and cloth are additional authored layers, not present in an unarmed human walk capture.

Use real sole vertices, material anchors and a declared ground/root model. Dense contact checks must include sub-key samples and a known bad perturbation. Captured ankle-to-ball pitch is not directly the boot's flat-sole angle: declare rest/flat reference and anatomical mapping. Increasing measured toe-off may expose incorrect source weights rather than a motion error. Never weaken the motion merely to hide stretched surfaces.

## Trace a rendered defect back to its cause

1. Freeze the exact bad cel, native size, view, camera components and pose. Inspect colour and silhouette with adjacent frames.
2. Inventory fixed source face/edge IDs and vertex owners. Colour diagnostics explain ownership; a blue face need not have three shin-owned vertices.
3. Raycast the exact orthographic pixel centre through the evaluated model. Record actual hit face, source rest coordinates, owners and pose. A screenshot alone cannot distinguish ankle geometry from a misassigned calf.
4. Measure fixed rest edge lengths versus evaluated lengths across the complete cycle. Separate rigid-piece edges from legitimate joint transitions. Restrict a patch to fixed source IDs and expected original weights; reject stale source hashes atomically.
5. Render actual matched before/after neutral, heel, passing, recovery and toe-off views. Confirm the bad surface disappears without new gaps, plates collapsing, source detail leaking or boots moving incorrectly. Rerun dense sole checks.

The local raytrace found apparent ankle horns on calf vertices at restZ approximately-.64..-.76: an old |X|<=.105 crotch exception assigned them to the thigh far below the knee. This was distinct from real foot/shin bridge stretching. Correcting the owner rule is appropriate before cutting new armour geometry. Preserve rejected patch attempts, including fixes that close one cuff seam but open another gap.

If actual rigid surfaces still stretch visibly, separate plates and author joint backing/endcaps as geometry. Declare changed topology and retained source UV/positions explicitly. Do not claim the original mesh was preserved after a cut. Do not use a camera-facing red card to hide a hole.

## Verify the assembled character

Preserve named attachment empties and once-only rest registration. Mathematical palm/grip coincidence is not evidence that fingers enclose a real handle. Inspect equipped silhouettes: the shield can hide the complete recovering leg even when attachment error is microscopic. Source-constrained carrying is legitimate; generic unarmed swing is not a requirement. Load, balance, arm reach and visual appeal need their own review.

Test real moving cape/body surfaces, not just cloth control targets or floor bounds. Local v4 showed326triangle overlap pairs and actual dark leg crescents through its lower hem. A fixed envelope correction requires anchor checks as well: a first improved module silently moved shoulder anchors5.7mm. Preserve failures; recheck after changing calf, carry or equipment. Zero sampled surface intersections is not continuous collision simulation or craft approval.

Pack the editable scene with its true playback range, source actions and textures. Reopen representative actual poses and independently append each standalone module. Restore exact camera position/scale/Euler and lights. In the shield test matrix decomposition changed nearest-texture samples by up to28RGBlevels despite tiny transform differences; restoring the original components reproduced every rendered RGBA byte. Subpixel world-projection tolerance and exact texture-render equality are separate gates.

Collect the union of existing action key times before authoring a replacement layer. Do not assume that integer frames cover the source. The articulated Captain had521 quarter-frame keys; initial carry/head replacements changed only131 integers. At33.25/33.5/33.75 the wrist targets reverted by258/128mm and the head lost its declared8degree correction, although all65 exported integer sprites and grip-marker checks passed. Preserve that failure. Author the intended layer at all source key times, and independently compare evaluated chest-relative wrists and world head rotation to their design targets. Grip coincidence alone cannot prove the arm pose. Keep arbitrary-time interpolation distinct from finite sampled checks. The newv7 package passes521-time authorship and unchanged lower/spine/neck checks; this fixes editable playback scope, not visual quality.

## Consume and review the final sprite

Verify action-slot binding after portable module exchange, including shape-key actions. In the wide-stance cape repair, fresh append retained exact bone matrices yet left the copied Key action unbound: LagLeft remained0 instead of.026 and the cloth differed7.69mm. Explicitly rebinding the original ActionSlot reproduced all521 evaluated cape poses exactly, with an exact rendered/reopened witness. Matching an action name or skeleton alone is insufficient.

Keep physical source space separate from an in-place preview controller. Dense sole checks on the packed preview initially reported453mm travel because its existing horizontal root cancellation was still active. Evaluate the physical scene by undoing only that cancellation on an unsaved audit copy. The world consumer subsequently restores the captured root displacement. Never silently cancel root motion in a plant test.

Treat a wider walking track as authored motion, not a camera fix. The tested125mm outward offset per foot changed eight leg tracks while preserving hips/root/upper tracks. It required fresh IK reach, all-rendered-volume ground checks and cape tests; one formerly clear cape intersected the wider boot. A fixed extra5mm hem profile cleared521 sampled poses while preserving the protected top band. Sparse improved poses still need a complete motion review for waddling, balance and weight.

Use the fixed bake camera to project actual root travel and ground grid. Test synchronized pose/root playback separately from continuous root motion during held cels. A nearby opaque beauty patch can test bounded within-held motion, but does not identify the same sole material across changing poses. Document that scope rather than calling it complete foot-plant proof.

Render true native256 and native384 if comparing detail. Label nearest display enlargement and resampled comparison explicitly. Higher resolution exposes defects; it does not improve gait. Keep body shader choices controlled against identical geometry/pose/camera. Unlit paint may preserve face detail but flatten volume; AO is a candidate, not automatically superior.

Verify the full review travel envelope at each native size and display zoom. The old1024px world stage clipped150 native384/nearest2x packets across three loops. Use one larger fixed canvas/origin for the entire export; never fit each cel or recenter the root to hide motion. Record stage size/origin in the receipt and reject actual clipped output. Freshv7 world evidence decodes2340 packets with no clipping; its separate Godot check uses normal lossless SpriteFrames imports,65 source-byte-identical PNGs and130 actual GPU readbacks on two backgrounds. These consumer checks grant no art points.

Independent adversarial review must identify exact source/export hashes, full adjacency/wrap coverage, native size and timed viewing mode. Actual decoded video frames are valid witnesses but not continuous video ingestion. Inspect full figure/support/recovery before ornament. A user rejection revokes previous aesthetic acceptance. Do not accumulate score points for repaired local defects or promote a technically passing clip to9/10.

After actual calf ownership, articulated boots, cape clearance, shield grip and521-time authoring repairs, two freshv7 reviews still rejected finished-game art: overall6.5 and6, motion6 in both. The surviving defects were recovering-leg occlusion, compact/tucked character presentation, muted loaded/release storytelling and uneven shell/collar/cape finish. A controlled35/45degree camera-yaw diagnostic reproduced all ten baseline cels exactly and showed that changing view alone did not create a useful leg gap. Treat that as a failed hypothesis, not a higher-score version. New walking-track or pose design must be an explicit authored motion layer, with fresh actual contact/equipment/art checks; preserving a human capture is not automatically correct for a heavily armoured character.

## Recent bounded lessons: inspect key poses before expansion

Require actual diagnostic report semantics, not only successful process exits. The first V8 consumer orchestration incorrectly treated a diagnostic's exit0 as acceptance despite a[-2,+1]pixel held-foot failure. Preserve the false-pass receipt, explicitly revoke it, and validate every evidence state and negative control. A coherent60Hz pose/root snapshot fixed35 tested within-held patch samples at[0,0], with an8pixel negative rejected, but introduces16.700mm visible lag against continuous source-root interpolation in this cycle. The ledger is external captured motion, not an implemented physics engine. Fixing this clock does not improve the underlying gait's art score.

Diagnose actual UV chart ownership before fine editing. Frontal and side eye features can occupy distant atlas islands. The compact Captain socket edits987 owned texels and replays exactly, while the earlier large plate was independently rejected as a dark mask. Render matched controls from the exact same scene/camera components: a1e-7 camera round-trip difference caused179-level nearest-filtered shield differences in initially mismatched controls. Preserve those failed controls. Owned source texels do not imply unchanged framebuffer pixels: indirect light and path sampling can affect unedited surfaces. Finite actual pixel rays and texture-neighbour tests support a bounded leakage diagnosis, not universal proof.

For source-attached shader masks, include edge/interior UV samples on actual triangles. A texel-centre ownership guard missed a nonsole subpixel triangle; one of1,589,808 Linear samples received mask.078125. Removing its guarded UV footprint and rerunning the same finite samples eliminated observed leakage. This is stricter empirical evidence, not an analytic every-subpixel guarantee. Sole-only emission is declared shader authorship and can change indirect illumination; do not call original shader or outside-framebuffer preservation.

Review representative support, extension, passing and recovery poses before a costly new full bake. The independently rejected general spine lean improved one bracing pose only slightly and did not repair the far-leg silhouette; it also introduced additional backgear contacts. Do not convert such component improvements into a new full-cycle score. Preserve whole mesh normals outside a bounded shape edit: Blender's whole-mesh custom-normal setter can alter untouched smooth fans. Compare actual stored and evaluated normals, not geometry fingerprints alone.

A historical first-person production reference is Thomas Vasseur's [Dead Cells 3D-to-2D workflow](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/art-design-deep-dive-using-a-3d-pipeline-for-2d-animation-in-i-dead-cells-i-), published2018. It prioritizes convincing, correctly timed key poses before adding/exporting more frames. This is established game-production evidence, not a recent AI result or a technique already proven by the Captain fixture. The local route uses detailed modernRGBA and a different renderer; do not imply it reproduces Dead Cells' shader, frame style or solved pixel flicker.

## Source diagnosis, review calibration and acceleration

Correct frozen interpretations with a new evidence record. Later exact knee rays
found all648 sampled rays hitting their own-side leg at four inspected poses;
the prior literal missing-knee description was unsupported there. Actual source103
was heel/toe transfer, not recovering swing. Internal joint projection and a
crowded silhouette do not establish surface occlusion. Preserve the original
review, link the correction, and do not grant an art score for ray visibility.

Independent whole reviews of the **same** V8 source gave6.5 and8, not a measured
version improvement. Record disagreement and shared defects rather than picking
the higher score or adding component points. Neither established9/10. Review
whole native colour and motion before assigning priorities from magnified crops;
one reviewer did not consider the dark outsole a blocker. The isolated lighter
palette and outward knee trials were rejected as meaningful repairs.

Preserving hip/ankle endpoints and segment lengths does not preserve the knee
hinge. Separate thigh/shin alignment altered the relative hinge15.693degrees in a
failed test. A common world rotation about the hip-to-ankle axis preserved the
relative basis while changing the bend plane; even then the strongest trial
looked splayed and was rejected. Check relative joint rotations as well as
endpoints, ground contacts and reach.

For portable mesh edits, protect stored corner normals **and** sharp-edge flags.
The normal encoder changed32 flags despite otherwise correct bounded geometry.
An explicit capsule restored flags, wrote only listed rest vertices and encoded
normals, and rejected stale inputs and double application before any writes.
Local normal fans may include unmoved adjacent vertices; list those exceptions.
If a fixed screen-space anchor loses its feature after an edit, report its metric
as undefined. It is not a successful zero-width result.

Shape-key values are dimensionless coefficients. Measure actual deformation in
declared rest/world space, check action slots and actual rotation modes, and
separate cloth deformation from mount/root movement. The cape's identity stored
quaternion did not imply stationary bones: their actual mode wasXYZ. Protect true
attachment geometry independently from shader masks; gold hem/side borders may
need to bend while their material remains protected. An authored lag is not a
cloth force simulation. Review chronological playback; translated stiff panels
can pass every contact check without convincing fabric motion.

Benchmark actual installed devices before increasing workers. The local bounded
CUDA RTX5090 probe rendered one warm native384 phase in0.727seconds versusCPU
2.919seconds, but initialization took6.919seconds. A fresh-process replay differed
at one pixel by1LSB; the exact gate failed. Keep exploratory GPU auditions separate
from authoritative pixel receipts, pin backend/samples/seed, retain failed replay,
and declare any tolerances before use. No saved global preference changes were
needed. GPU Blender rendering does not establish GPU Godot rendering. Reusable
commands and actual evidence are in
`pilot/captain-renderer-benchmark-v1/README.md` in the local construction lab.

Render a complete inexpensive visual audition before an authoritative export when
static edits remain ambiguous. The tested fixed35mm rear/±15mm lateral cape-hem
lag passed all521 sampled body-cape checks and preserved300 attachment vertices,
yet both independent reviewers retained the original crease baseline after
inspecting actual65-pose normal/half decoded cycles and wrap. Translating the
lower part of a stiff panel is not demonstrated fabric animation. A following
test needs spatially different fold/edge deformation and a readable return; do
not simply add points for more movement or larger amplitudes.

Use a synchronized original-PNG review player when evaluating source quality:
one shared rational clock, native/nearest display sizes, pause/step/seek and exact
variant frame registration. The local
`pilot/captain-cape-review-player-v1` fixture verifies frozen inputs before copying,
opens offline, and has130 actual Chromium source-composite readbacks plus clock,
wrap, keyboard, zoom and wall-time-play checks. Browser-composite rounding has an
explicit±1RGB bound. This validates the review tool, not the animation's quality,
Godot integration or world-contact policy. Preserve the rejected audition and
source-bound opinions alongside the next candidate.

Trace the material actually visible before iterating cloth shape or contrast.
In the Captain case, evaluated camera rays identified the solidified cape's
front lining as materialslot1 at all four inspected phases. Most of that surface
received almost no contrast from the crease attribute; an older darkening node
was orphaned. Increasing edge curl changed the hem but left the exposed panel
flat. The independent review therefore recommended one fixed UV value painting
test, rather than another whole-panel translation.

Validate UV ownership in source coordinates, including image row orientation.
The actual protected top150 vertices mapped to Blender v0..0.15625, while PNGtop
maps to Blender v1. A thin sourcegold row fell just inside the nominal v.96
guard: the preflight caught it, and explicit sourcegold ownership preserved it.
Freeze the recipe before output, use smooth transitions into the owned region,
and reject stale source, protected RGBA leakage and alpha changes before writing.
Keep the original packed images and original material graphs; declare the new
front binding as an exception and preserve the rear binding independently.

This front-paint fixture passed521 sampled pose/geometry/source checks and exact
packed CPU reopen, but rendered alpha and back-view pixels changed slightly.
Filtering, indirect light and adaptive sampling can change framebuffers despite
exact source alpha or rear data; do not claim outside-pixel preservation. The
bounded before/after views establish only a component trial. Full-cycle review,
fresh engine consumption and whole-art acceptance remain separate gates.

Compare motion on the same anatomical basis before diagnosing exaggeration.
The Captain contact-conditioning layer raised knee peaks from65–69 to86–90
degrees; later stance widening contributed almost no vertical ankle excursion.
An ipsilateral hip-relative comparison normalized by leg length confirmed this
change. It does not prove that all extra lift is inappropriate: pitched rigid
boots require support height, unlike a human foot with articulating toes.

Test a causal hypothesis independently before combining repairs. A floor-safe
maximum-height ablation passed floor/reach checks and slightly reduced peak
knee bend, yet raised ankles locally by120mm and prolonged recovery with32
degrees more bend. Reject that candidate as a gait improvement; report signed
per-phase changes, not just peaks. A separate fixed swing-only pitch damping
test reduced local bend up to6.5degrees while preserving stance/root/hips, but
still needs actual articulated-mesh, contact, render and art review. Skeleton
feasibility is a preparation gate, not evidence that game animation improved.

Keep source timing limitations visible. C0 seam conditioning does not prove C1
velocity continuity; measure one-sided residuals without inventing perfect-loop
claims. Equipped guard motion can already contain authored load response despite
quiet arms. Do not demand unarmed arm swing as a universal quality requirement.
