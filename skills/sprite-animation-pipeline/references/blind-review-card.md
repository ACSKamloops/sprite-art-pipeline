# Anonymous sprite review card

Use only the nominated anonymous packet, its frozen input ledger, the user's style brief/reference and this rubric. Do not open producer reports, source mappings, prior scores, case histories or STATE files. Record accidental exposure and its effect on independence. Freeze the review before unblinding.

Compare at native game scale and nearest enlargement on contrasting backgrounds. Include every pose, every predecessor/current pair and wrap. Check character identity, costume/material volumes, joint attachment, limb roles, visible support/swing progression, silhouette, alpha edges and framing. Occlusion can legitimately hide toes, hands or gaps; perspective does not require mirrored step silhouettes. Name actual evidence when alleging an anatomical or phase failure.

Assess the full declared timeline. Distinguish held poses, interpolated frames, changed exposure timing and a changed drawing. Inspect actual presentation timestamps and loop boundaries. Reuse supplied mechanical verification where sufficient; spend review effort on the visible result rather than duplicating thousands of assertions. If continuous video perception is unavailable, state that motion judgments reconstruct chronology from decoded stills and known holds. Do not claim human normal-speed playback or controller feel.

Judge drawing/material/anatomy, motion/readability, temporal identity and practical use separately. Successful exports, increased frame count and source-pixel proofs do not earn art points. A timing-only revision does not earn new drawing points. A color-only revision cannot change an identical underlying gait. Use the user's intended art style rather than imposing limited-palette or retro conventions.

Give an honest scoped overall judgment and uncertainty. Do not invent defects to justify a low score, or round a result upward to satisfy a producer target. Distinguish a demonstrated defect from a performance preference or a hypothesis requiring human playback. For each material issue, give a source frame/pair, coordinates where useful, expected visual benefit and the smallest appropriate next experiment. No confident preference is a valid result.

Bind the report to input fingerprints, covered clips, scales, backgrounds, timing and actual perception limits. One reviewed walk does not certify other directions, equipment, actions, transitions or physical foot contact. Changed art or timing needs a new scoped review; previous acceptance never silently transfers.
