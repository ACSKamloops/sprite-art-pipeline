# Proportion repair with painted part masters

Use this case when repeated boot paint/foreshortening changes make a compact
diagonal humanoid walk difficult to follow. The portable example is
`examples/lyra-leg-repair` in the sprite-art-pipeline repository. It supplements
the existing manual-gait reference; it is not an accepted whole-animation recipe.

Cleaner generated boots did not repair the passing pose: V1 still knotted the
visible thigh/cuff branches and introduced an isolated long side-on toe at the
wrap. A later same-master cloth/calf/foot reconstruction improved repeated
material identity but crowded both boots together and hid thigh length.

The useful bounded correction increased visible trouser area, narrowed/tapered
the shaft and raised the returning boot through the opposite support exchange.
The exact authored projected controls, source owners and inverse nearest maps
are retained. They are not recovered joints, physical foot contacts or universal
anthropometric ratios. The appearance change must be reviewed on the whole actor.

Preserve a fixed whole-clip canvas and exact clock for the comparison. Check the
actual source image/part origin before blaming a target trajectory. Protecting
upper pixels proves localization only. A brief toe overlap can be legitimate;
prolonged same-height crowding can hide the exchange. Do not force uniform motion
or remove every stance hold to maximize difference metrics.

The V4 history-aware reviewer found an ordered-still improvement with no new
blocking connection gap, after every pose/pair/wrap. It deliberately left normal-
speed stiffness, seam catches and the slimmer design to human playback. No8/10
or9/10 score, physical planting or game replacement was established.

For native editor exchange, transparent RGB normalization needs an explicit
receipt. This case's toolkit renderer zeroed16 fully transparent source pixels;
the PNG authority retained them. Compare actual visible/partial-alpha bytes and
record hidden-only differences rather than claiming unqualified fullRGBA equality.
