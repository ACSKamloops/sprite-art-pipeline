Hair color, costume theme, and exact weapon are secondary to proportions, camera, material treatment, and feature readability. A comparable reference need not clone Lyra. It also need not have eight frames or eight directions: accept only the action/view actually inspected and label missing coverage.

Screening decisions should use plain roles: "primary appearance and walk comparator," "appearance match; motion/action still unverified," "secondary technique only," or "unsuitable for this target." State the concrete reason and name a frame witness. Do not assign a subjective match percentage. A candidate can clear appearance and still fail motion quality or action equivalence. Continuous playback acceptance must remain separate from ordered-still inspection.
