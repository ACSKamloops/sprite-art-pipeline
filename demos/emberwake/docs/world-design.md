# Emberwake: The Hollow Bell — world and journey

The valley is a 2304 × 1728 world shown through a 960 × 540 camera. The village opens in the southwest; three connected woodland clearings lead to a shrine in the northeast. Pale ground tiles form continuous routes. Trees leave at least 100 pixels around route centerlines and clear 205-pixel ward arenas. Sprite feet share a Y-sorted layer; static feet collisions occupy layer 1. Scenic ponds have solid elliptical shorelines. The shrine's visible threeward seal has a physical collider until all three wards are kindled.

The title offers Begin or Continue. A saved journey requires a separate explicit replacement choice before New begins. A brief introduction leads to Aster, whose dialogue explains controls, mana, flasks, safe rests and the three wards. The Moss clearing contains three scouts; Tide has a brute and three scouts; Crown has two brutes and three scouts. Encounters begin only when approached. Defeated creatures release embers that restore a little health and mana. Clearing each encounter enables E at its ward, which restores the hero, replenishes both flasks, records the checkpoint and lights the stone. The three wards open the shrine. Crossing its approach records a shrine checkpoint and wakes the Rootbound Warden. Only defeating the Warden enables the ending interaction at the Hollow Bell. After the ending, the player can explore and return to Aster for a final exchange.

Walking, combat and resources belong to `hero.gd` and `enemy.gd`. Main connects their public signals and owns quest state, pausing, dialogue, healing charge validation, map collision, encounter setup, checkpoint recovery, sound, saves and title/ending flow. The HUD paints resource bars, objective, interaction prompt, combat keys, compass, journal map and modal panels; menu controls are real keyboard-focusable Buttons. Esc pauses simulation and opens the journal. E/Enter advances dialogue. Muting is exposed in the journal and saved with the journey.

The version-1 save at `user://emberwake-save.json` records only validated progression, checkpoint key, elapsed time and separate music/effects preferences. It does not resume midway through attacks. Continue restores full resources at the saved safe checkpoint and rebuilds unfinished encounters; death keeps kindled wards and restarts unfinished combat. Saves occur on new-game confirmation, Aster conversation, resting, kindling, shrine entry, boss defeat, completion, Save & title, and periodic play. Invalid versions, wrong field types and impossible checkpoint/progression combinations are rejected. Writes use a temporary file and rename. Test mode uses a separate `user://emberwake-test-save.json`.

`--test-mode` is reported by `get_debug_state()` but never auto-completes ordinary gameplay. Tests can drive the same `_menu_action`, dialogue, hero action and interaction methods and inspect `get_debug_state()`. It exposes mode, hero position, ward and encounter flags, foes, boss state, checkpoint, objective, flasks, elapsed time and save validity. The map placement uses a fixed seed so geometry is reproducible.

Art is loaded from the shared contract's `assets/world` files with authored foot pivots, and all actors use the generated art owned by their respective systems. Ground fallback colors only support development before imports. Audio paths are `assets/audio/music.ogg` and `assets/sfx/{ward,pickup,ui,death}.wav`; systems play combat effects on the shared SFX bus. Optional typefaces are `assets/fonts/{body,title}.ttf`.

Intended first-play length is approximately 10–15 minutes, depending on combat and reading pace; this is a design target, not a measured player-study result. The world is one connected playable chapter with a finite ending and continued exploration afterward.
