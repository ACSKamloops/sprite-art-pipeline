# Frozen cold paired review: Rowan southeast walk

**Prefer B, by a modest margin.** Its fuller trouser-to-boot shapes at keys 03–05, especially 04, are easier to understand at native and game size on all three backgrounds. Both preserve the same strong character identity. Neither has validated continuous motion or floor contact in this review.

These are separate subjective scores. Motion readability means the information in the drawings and adjacent poses; it is not a playback score. Anchors: 0 fails the category, 5 is a serviceable prototype with clear weaknesses, and 10 is exceptionally clear and polished for this small painted RPG use. File count, effort, maps, hashes and review quantity earn no art points. No overall score is calculated.

A has appealing auburn hair, a readable blue eye, convincing silver highlights and a clear blue/gold costume. The weak point is the lower body: the pinched cuff at 03, narrow crossing leg shape at 04 and notched pale cuff/rear boot at 05 reduce its material solidity and step clarity. It is credible prototype art, with visible cleanup still needed.

B keeps those strengths and improves the three troublesome keys. The clearest improvement is the diagonal thigh into a substantial forward boot at 04. It is a better base for the intended walk. Its compressed feet at 03 and broad cream knee band at 05 remain crowded, and the changing thigh shape through 04–06 still needs attention. This is not a polished-motion pass.

The supplied reference and unscored south/east walk and idle art support both candidates as the same character: spiky auburn hair, silver shoulder plates, blue/gold tunic, cream trousers and brown boots. The southeast face and torso direction are plausible between those cardinal views. This is static identity/turn context only; no animated turn was evaluated.

## Inspection and limits

Only REVIEW-CARD.md, INPUTS.json and the 37 listed images were read as inputs. No producer folder, parent mapping, other review or wider history was consulted. All 37 manifest image hashes matched before and after inspection. The card and manifest are also hashed in source-hash-ledger.json and SOURCE-SHA256SUMS. Keys 00, 01, 02, 06 and 07 are byte-identical across the candidates; 03–05 differ. That fact establishes scope only and contributes no quality points.

All 18 candidate × scale × background combinations were visually inspected in both eight-key sheets and complete cyclic-pair sheets. Native uses 128×128; nearest 2x uses 256×256; requested .66 uses rounded 84×84, an actual stage factor of .65625. Composites use uniform light #eee8dc, dark #1f262e and muted olive ground #696f4f. The ground is a contrast proxy, not a map. No source art was repainted or edited. All 20 cardinal images were viewed at native in context-native-light.png, and the supplied reference was viewed directly.

The manifest specifies 9/160 second per key and 9/20 second per loop. I did not perceive or test continuous playback. Smoothness, weight transfer, foot locking, sliding, cadence, bounce and loop popping remain unverified. A narrow or awkward join is a visible drawing concern, not proof that a limb is disconnected or that contact is physically wrong. No engine, translation speed, markers, collision data or textured scene was inspected. Real engine scaling and anchoring may differ.

Confidence is high for identity, medium for appearance and practical suitability, and lower for runtime motion. Decimal scores express the judgment, not measurement certainty. B is preferred for further validation because its leg structure is visibly clearer; both contain usable art, so the evidence does not warrant rejecting both.
