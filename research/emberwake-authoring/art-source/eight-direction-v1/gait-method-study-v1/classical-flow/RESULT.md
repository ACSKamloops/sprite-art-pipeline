# Rejected classical interpolation pilot

The four variants are DIS/Farneback with either nearest-source selection plus alpha0 hole filling or premultiplied blending of nearest samples. All original eight cels remain exact at every fourth sample; cycle remains9/20s. This is classical estimation, no learned model and no H3.

Inspection at128/256 on light/dark grounds finds doubled/translucent soles in the blend branches (especially03→04), and speckled pinched leather or added contour fragments in the source branches. The source branch hole fill can expose a second donor silhouette. The underlying opposite-support/body weight story is unchanged. These are root observations from stills, not a cold independent or continuous human review.

Reject all four before engine packaging. The experiment does not establish that every possible optical-flow method fails; it establishes that these exact parameters/ownership rules are unsuitable. More samples are not a quality score.

Implemented against the official [OpenCV dense-flow definition](https://docs.opencv.org/5.0/main_modules/video_track.html) and [inverse remap documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/4.13.0/da/d54/group__imgproc__transform.html), retrieved2026-10-16, undated technical references.
