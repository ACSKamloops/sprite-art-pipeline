## Entry, volume and progression

**04→05 has a cost.** The boot that will be lifted in 06 already rises and opens outward in B05. This gives a more distinct lift from 04 than A's compact 04/05 grouping. I would not call that entry gradual or smooth. The cuff/trouser orientation changes fairly sharply, though the new shape remains a plausible overlapped leg rather than an obvious anatomical swap. Screen side alone was not used to identify a leg.

**05→06 improves.** A05 keeps the boots close and comparatively low, then opens them sharply in 06. B05 places the rounded lifted boot at an intermediate height/attitude before 06 while the lower boot remains a substantial volume. Following the painted cuff and boot planes, the upper/side surfaces now progress into 06 with less sudden rearrangement. B05 is not simply a cut-down patch of brown: the calf/cuff and broad rounded toe remain legible.

The gain survives native and 84 px on light and muted backgrounds. On dark ground some dark trouser/cuff detail still merges, but the changed outside boot silhouette remains readable. The advantage comes from a more useful intermediate pose and coherent boot planes, not from having a larger gap. A legitimate passing overlap need not leave the feet permanently separated.

**06→07 is unchanged.** The previously accepted 06 remains exact and continues into the same 07 stride. This edit earns no second award for that existing benefit. The new 05 leads into it more clearly, but the broad opening into 07 remains part of the sequence.

The entry tradeoff and remaining joints keep game usefulness at 6.75. I give a small motion-readability increase for the more legible 05→06 progression, without raising appearance: the new cel does not establish an overall finish improvement.
