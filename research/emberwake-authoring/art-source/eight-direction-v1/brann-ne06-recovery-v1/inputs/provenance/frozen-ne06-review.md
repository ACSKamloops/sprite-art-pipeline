## Actual change and tradeoff

**06→07 improves.** A06 presents a large sole-facing brown mass close to the other boot, followed by a sudden opening into 07's diagonal stride. In B06, the boot masses are more distinct and the lifted boot presents a side/upper plane that follows more convincingly into 07. Following the painted cuff paths, the lower boot can extend into the long diagonal of 07 while the lifted boot remains on the same readable side of the grouping. This reduces the sole flash and the apparent foot-plane turn. NE and NW show the same advantage.

At native and 84 px, B06 reads more readily as two differently posed boots; A06 is easier to read as one knotted central mass. On dark ground some brown/dark-gray separation still disappears, but the benefit remains in the outside silhouette. This is a visual path judgment from ordered poses; it is not observed continuous playback.

**05→06 is a tradeoff.** B opens the space between the boots more suddenly than A's compact 05/06 grouping. That makes the entry less gradual in spacing. However, B's boot planes and distinct silhouettes make the subsequent path easier to infer. I judge the exit improvement larger than this entry cost, without calling either transition smooth.

**06 joints and finish are still limited.** The lower hanging boot connects through a narrow, hooked knee/cuff bend. It is continuous rather than visibly detached, but the sudden direction change feels pinched, especially at 2x. The dark trousers and boot shading do not describe that bend as clearly as they could. The larger lifted boot remains broad and rounded; foreshortening can explain the width, so width alone is not a fault. I see no newly clipped sole, isolated old sole fragment, extra floating boot piece, or transparent hole enclosed inside a knee. The large gray side projection is less conspicuous than in A; the remaining gray contour can read as a hip/cuff edge and is not an obvious separate remnant at 84 px.

That balance leaves appearance at 7.0 rather than awarding finish points merely because a foot was moved or repainted. Identity remains 9.0: the head, gold armor, blue/gold outfit and orange rear sash agree with the supplied appearance and N/E/W turning context. Both walks still face the expected rear diagonals.
