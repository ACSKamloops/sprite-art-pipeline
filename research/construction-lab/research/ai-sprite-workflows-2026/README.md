No model was downloaded, paid service used, or quality benchmark reproduced by this research task.

**Recommended pilot method (inference):** generate/approve one original master and missing hidden part drawings; keep an editable ordered part stack; author named joints, stable ground/root reference and equipment grip; animate explicit anticipation, extension and recovery keys; use IK during declared contact phases; bake deterministic pixel frames; reserve AI for master generation or localized material corrections. Review joint gaps, silhouette, gear attachment and weight at native size and during real timed playback. Numeric consistency gates are necessary diagnostics, not production-art approval.
