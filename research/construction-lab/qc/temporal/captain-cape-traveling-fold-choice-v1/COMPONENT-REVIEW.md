The candidate changes the visible lower edge slightly and adds small differences in the panel's shading/curvature. I independently decoded and visually viewed every first-cycle frame and first wrap from the actual baseline and candidate normal/half MP4s: four streams, indices 0–65, **264 decoded images total**. A technically differential deformation is not automatically a perceptually useful traveling fold. I do not find a conspicuous new visual defect; the issue is insufficient benefit.
