# Bow carry walk: limited foundation acceptance

**Accepted as a four-view modern RPG prerendered walk foundation only.** The bow makes the restrained right-arm performance purposeful. This is a convincing carried-object walk benchmark, not a finished original CopperScout design, pixel-painted art, user art-direction approval, or approval of other animations.

Grip stays associated with the same anatomical hand through front/right/back/left. Near/far occlusion changes logically with the view; I observed no attachment jump, ownership swap, costume identity pop or gross loop reset. The body preserves recognizable volume and alternating bent/lifted passing knees. In the reviewed key images the lower bow limb appears above the lowest foot regions rather than dragging below them. That is a projected-image observation, not a measured world-ground clearance guarantee.

The remaining limits are visible. Thin olive bow limbs lose contrast on the dark audition background, especially behind the character where much of the curve is occluded. Feet are narrow and the face remains sparse. The arms express restrained bow carry, not relaxed unarmed swing. Left-facing exposed forearm/calves are noticeably brighter than right-facing equivalents. Cameras orbit the same studio scene and lamps: this produces coherent object-relative studio shading, but does not prove lighting consistency under a fixed game camera as a character turns. Style/background review must accept that baked difference or require a new lighting treatment.
