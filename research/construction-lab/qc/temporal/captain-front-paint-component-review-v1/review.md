# Independent front-paint art review

Decision: the fixed front painting gives a modest meaningful art gain, sufficient to run a full65 motion audition. This approves further evaluation, not final cloth art or a high whole-character rating.

At native384 the exposed cream wedge under the shield now separates into a darker inner trough and a lighter outer face. This is visible in all four whole-character pairs, with source65 and93 giving useful comparisons. At native256 the cue survives weakly; the improvement is easier to identify in a matched pair than on first glance. It reduces the blank beige-sheet appearance while preserving the recognizable Captain, red armor, gold shield and cream/gold fabric. No distracting new painted blotch is evident in these views.

The improvement is smaller than the strong banding seen on the512 texture preview. Most texture area is hidden by shield or body, so texture appearance cannot substitute for whole-sprite quality. Standalone whole384 source65 and whole256 source93 were also inspected to confirm the finding without composite labels or neighboring sprites.

Remaining defects:

- The outer gold border and bottom edge remain long and straight. The painted light face can still read as one flat panel bounded by a rodlike edge rather than thick soft cloth rolling into a weighted hem.
- The shadow bands are broad and smooth. They add volume cues but lack the master's distinct angular pale/shadow planes and localized crease hierarchy. At256 a diffuse gradient loses more descriptive work than a purposeful crease.
- Back93 remains a broad trapezoid with faint parallel shading. No major rear cloth improvement is evident. Credit this candidate only for the exposed lining it changes.
- The texture shows regularly spaced vertical bands with unequal darkness. Unequal darkness helps, but equal spacing risks a curtain-like striped read as more surface is revealed. Four witnesses do not establish whether that risk appears in motion.

Hold this candidate fixed for a full65 comparison. Examine whole native384 frames and normal/half actual decoded chronology including the encoded wrap, with native256 checks at the four key poses. Determine whether the legible trough remains attached to cloth as shield/leg reveals change, without becoming a persistent dark stripe or disappearing. Check that the lighter face remains visually joined to its gold border. No larger flutter or arm swing is needed.

I formed the visual decision from master, paired whole256/384, back93 and source/paint/guard images before reading README and recipe. Those documents were then read for ownership, not another rating. The recipe binds the new image only to owned front lining and leaves rear material data intact. This reviewer did not open the blend in Blender or independently recertify geometry preservation.
