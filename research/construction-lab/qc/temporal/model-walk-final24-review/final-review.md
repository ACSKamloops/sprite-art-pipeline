# Guarded walk: limited benchmark acceptance

**Accepted only as a four-view guarded-walk prerendered benchmark.** It is a credible improvement in actual movement and dimensional anatomy, not approval of the rejected CopperScout library, a finished new character design, or the user's art direction.

The current pack has convincing hood/head, shoulder, torso and pelvis volume through four real views. The swing leg bends and lifts during passing, the alternating strides remain legible, and the body responds with a controlled bob. Chronological browser states show the same performance repeating at the corrected 1.25-second cadence without the previous halfcycle hop, cutout joint gaps or obvious reset. Brighter calves/feet and160px native rendering help leg separation. Frontal face marks improve structure, with no obvious eye marks bleeding onto hood or arms in the inspected poses.

The performance is restrained. Hands stay in a relatively narrow forward/hanging corridor while the legs stride; it reads as a cautious guarded or equipment-carry walk, not a loose empty-handed stroll. Feet remain narrow, and facial personality is limited. These are real design/performance limits to preserve in the description; a relaxed or more appealing heroic gait needs a separately authored arm/shoulder performance. The low-poly prerendered appearance is distinct from pixel-painted art and is subject to the user's style choice.

What changed versus prior failures is the source of the pose quality: this is an artist-authored licensed rig/Walk performance rendered from true camera orientations. It avoids inconsistent independent AI-pose anatomy/floor positions, V2 tube-limb/card-like view changes, and painted-cutout short-arm/joint seams. The rendering pipeline preserved that authored performance; continuity checks alone did not create or approve it. This is not evidence that arbitrary AI art can be automatically rigged into equally good motion.

All48 source poses,44 ordinary adjacent comparisons and four loop wraps at native1x and exact nearest2x. The v7 timing claim was superseded, never accepted.

I then viewed **all46 actual chronological browser screenshots** (23native,23nearest2x; four synchronized views each) and **all12 separate paused-pose screenshots**, arranged as22 bounded strips. Browser clock logs completed at least three1.25s cycles per scale and stopped consistently on pause. Screenshot before/after states bracket capture; a picture can span a pose boundary. I did **not** continuously ingest video, and temporal stills cannot replace a human's direct playback/art-direction judgment.

The fixed-origin audition does not prove moving-world stance grip, movement-speed calibration, contact/toe semantics, gameplay backgrounds, collision windows, other actions or transitions. No such claim is made from bone/foot controls. Other equipment/action/direction bindings are not approved by this walk receipt.
