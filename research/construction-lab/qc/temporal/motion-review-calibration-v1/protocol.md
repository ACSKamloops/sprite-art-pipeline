# Whole-motion review calibration after the Captain rejection

This protocol replaces threshold chasing with evidence of readable motion. My v20 9.025 acceptance was withdrawn in `e1a5b02`; its original receipt remains historical. A fixed numerical gate did not prevent overvaluing local repairs. This document approves no candidate.

These dates are printed publication dates, not crawler dates. I read the official written teaching pages. Their embedded video fetches were throttled; I have **not** viewed these tutorial performances in continuous real time. They are linked references for direct viewing, not a falsely claimed personal visual benchmark. No exact Captain combat-carry reference was verified in this bounded research pass. Local native Mana walks may serve later as a secondary small-game-sprite readability control; they are not anatomically or stylistically equivalent to this Captain.

The following criteria are my proposed review procedure, informed by those sources. They are not publisher-certified formulas, measured forces, or guarantees of quality.

1. Freeze and identify the **delivered artifact**, source manifest and intended action/view. Probe the actual video FPS/duration; decode its displayed frames and compare their source clock. Separate wrong export from poor source motion. Preserve failed versions as controls.
2. Review the whole figure **before ornament and seams**. Read the sequence as a silhouette or simple mass diagram at native size: which leg receives support, where does the pelvis travel, and how does the opposite foot become free? If that story is unclear, a gorgeous source painting does not compensate.
3. Mark contact, absorption/down, passing and release/up only where they are actually visible. Labels in a manifest are not evidence. Check both sides. Distinct functions matter more than eight evenly spaced keys or prescribed bob amplitudes. A conservative flat boot and a restrained armored gait can be legitimate.
4. Compare the loaded pose with the next passing/release pose. The chest, pelvis and support leg must communicate a coherent change of support at the delivered scale. Reject a permanent crouch whose feet paddle beneath an essentially retained hero pose, or a global oscillation presented as weight transfer. Do not diagnose exact center of mass or foot forces from 2D appearance.
5. Review carry-specific behavior. A raised sword and shield constrain free arm swing; they should remain held, balanced and congruent with the torso. Do not demand a generic unarmed swing. Conversely, intact grip is only attachment evidence: it does not prove the whole motion feels alive.
6. Review actual travel on a stable ground reference, plus the uncluttered in-place loop. Distinguish a sole-material slip from camera follow or registration. Do not recenter individual frames. Rendered floor/contact tests and the visual impression of support are separate evidence.
7. Inspect every displayed adjacency and the wrap for discontinuity, changing limb identity, volume collapse and texture drift. Then return to the **whole chronological cycle**, not just the worst local seam. A clean wrap and connected joints establish continuity; they do not establish appeal.
8. Inspect the final exported sequence at native size and exact nearest 2x, then obtain direct real-time human viewing of several cycles. Record precisely who watched what. My tool-based decoded sheets are not continuous video ingestion. If only sheets and sparse screenshots were available, report a diagnostic or provisional opinion; do not claim a definitive temporal acceptance from them.
9. Record positive and negative evidence with timestamps, source pose IDs and whole-silhouette observations. Use `reject`, `provisional`, or `scoped candidate acceptable` with reasons; no target score, no incremental points for repairs. User acceptance remains separate. A failed whole-motion read is not fixed merely because every requested local change was implemented.
