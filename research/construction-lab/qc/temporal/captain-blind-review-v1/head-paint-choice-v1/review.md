# Bounded source-head paint choice

**Reject the authored-temple-plate as the final treatment. Detail sharpening alone is also insufficient to resolve the head identity.** This is a three-phase relative art judgment, not a new whole-walk score.

I viewed the actual matched head and whole-character comparisons at native256 and384, source1/33/103, against the original nearest512 master. The plate does make the eye region easier to notice, and remains in a similar place in the three poses. However, its broad dark area runs from the eye across the side temple and down toward the cheek/ear. In source1 and33 particularly, it merges with the rear dark head area into a mask-like patch. At native256 whole-character size the dark patch is visible, but a small metal optic and separate lens are not clear. Native384 exposes the same problem rather than curing it. A dark diagonal patch is not enough to establish convincing metal planes.

The master instead has a compact, crisply bounded steel optic beside exposed facial skin, with distinct small dark socket and light metal rim. The candidate's red mark is subordinate to the much larger dark area. The unchanged rounded blond hair/profile remains a separate source-identity issue; neither variant corrects it.

Detail-sharpen slightly increases local facial contrast in the enlarged crops, but whole-character differences are slight. It does not establish a crisp lens/rim or materially improve the hair crest. I do not see grounds to call it a major noise failure from these images; it is simply too weak a repair for the stated problem.

Next bounded repair: use the optic centre and existing safe side-chart ownership, substantially shrink the plate's lower/rear coverage, restore exposed temple/cheek skin, and concentrate contrast into a compact light steel rim surrounding a dark socket with a tiny red lens. Give the plate two deliberate metal values instead of one large dark silhouette. Match the compact area of the master before adding circuitry. Keep the front-chart and side-chart feature transitions coherent across these same three poses. Preserve UV ownership, outside texels, alpha, geometry and source bone poses; do not invent a larger implant merely to make it noticeable. Native256 whole-character readability is the deciding view.
