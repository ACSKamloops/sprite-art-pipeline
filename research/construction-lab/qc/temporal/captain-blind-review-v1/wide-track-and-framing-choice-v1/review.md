# Bounded independent wide-track and presentation choice

**Choose125mm as the next whole-cycle experiment, conditional on the existing cape failure being repaired and rechecked.**50mm is too small a change to resolve the critical second recovery. This is a relative choice from sparse poses, not a whole-motion rating or approval; the frozen v7 review1a0737be is unchanged.

At50mm the first-half poses cel8/16/24/32 show more separation, but cels46/51/56/60 still substantially read as one advancing leg plus concealed recovery. At125mm, cel41 exposes two lower-leg contours clearly; cels46/49/51/56 show a more distinct far boot and its progression behind the flame leg. This is a real readability gain in colour and the silhouette contour, though cels46/51 still lack a generous open gap and the far knee is mostly covered by near leg and gear. Cel60 still largely collapses into one column. The repair is partial, not a complete solution to release/passing/support readability.

The125mm poses do not look obviously goofy or grossly bowlegged in these nine samples. Cel16 and24 form a broader, credible armoured base, closer to the original static master's separated heavy boots than the narrow visible v7 column. The original master is an identity/proportion reference, not walking ground truth. The torso remains narrow relative to the shield and the legs now have a broader straddle; a waddling performance or insufficient pelvis movement over the feet could emerge across the missing phases. In particular, the very similar retained upper guard over the widened legs could make them look detached from the loaded chest. Whole-cycle normal/slower world replay is required to choose this permanently. Additional widening is not justified by this evidence.

The input125mm variant has a reported actual boot/cape intersection at source45/cel22 (18 pairs). I viewed its separate384 failure render: cape/hem crowds the lifted flame-side lower leg under the shield, although the tiny triangle intersection is not reliably diagnosable from that raster alone. This is an existing geometry failure, not an acceptable tradeoff for visibility. A proposed5/10/20mm fixed rear-hem change has not been reviewed; no credit is given to a hypothetical repair. After a real correction, recheck all relevant poses and show the hem in colour and silhouette. Toe/sole numerical passes neither resolve this collision nor establish an attractive loaded walk.

## Separate fixed presentation choice

The master-and-fixed-cameras-native256 sheet at source1/33/99 makes fixed12° elevation/ortho2.7 a reasonable next presentation control. The body occupies more useful native pixels and reads more upright, with less overhead head/shoulder foreshortening.8° adds little useful improvement and further exposes the dark undersoles. Retain original yaw24.7024° rather than the previously unsuccessful35/45° quarter rotation.128 samples/no denoise is a sensible cleaner rendering control, and Linear texture sampling remains reasonable on the supplied evidence; this is not a claim of whole-cycle shader quality.

The larger black sole at source33 remains prominent in12° and8°; that is a visible source volume/material/art problem, not repaired by tighter framing. Face/cyber-eye, shoulder ornaments and the broad cream ankle cuff still depart from the master. The master’s purposeful dark clusters, compact red/gold shapes and outlined layered cape remain richer than the smooth lit model. Enlarging the render makes those differences more visible rather than correcting them.
