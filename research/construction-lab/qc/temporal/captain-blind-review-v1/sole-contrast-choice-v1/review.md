# Two-pose native256 sole palette choice

**No compelling native-size improvement; retain the existing .4 component as baseline rather than promoting this palette as a meaningful sole repair.** The contrast variant is not a gross visual failure. It stays dark and does not become a conspicuous grey slab or noisy artificial pattern. Its benefit is simply too small for the stated native256 readability problem.

At source33, the enlarged crop shows a slightly clearer charcoal plane and separation from the dark transverse groove. At source103 the exposed underside is still essentially a dark band beneath the red toe. Actual native256 whole images are nearly indistinguishable in their sole read: the raised boot continues to present a broad dark underside with weak internal structure. This does not resolve the black-oval objection. The source33 crop improvement is not enough to claim a meaningful whole-character gain.

Both controls already include the selected fill.4 and compact eye; only the owned three-value sole palette differs. I do not attribute the head, pose or prior tread improvement to this test. Keeping the same mask/layout/geometry is a controlled experiment, not an art benefit. Further testing should target a deliberate readable edge/groove hierarchy at actual256, possibly a bounded layout/edge-width treatment. Raising all three values again is not an established solution. Preserve the dark sole material and actual contact geometry; do not add decorative fine stripes to compensate for low native legibility.
