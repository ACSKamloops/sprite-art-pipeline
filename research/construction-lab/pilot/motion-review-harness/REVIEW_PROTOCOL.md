# Full-cycle motion review protocol

The user rejected Captain v20's animation despite earlier numerical reviews. Those reviews remain historical; they are not a permission to accept a new experiment. A successful codec, hash check or engine suite is not evidence of convincing gait.

## Before judging

Freeze source PNGs, ordered phase labels, exact positive integer holds, total cycle time, source origin/canvas and root/floor/camera policy. Identify whether support metadata is measured material identity, authored intention or merely a witness. An extrema point is not a boot-material proof. Do not repair root drift by separately moving feet, fitting each pose or hiding a defect beneath cloth.

Keep the real motion reference available with its source/timing bound. State which reference was actually watched: a real armoured/person walk, native game playback, or source animation. A still diagram or remembered gait is not a watched reference. Compare the earlier failed v20 at the same apparent actor scale and normal speed, but do not demand imitation of its defects or transfer its score.

## Actual playback order

1. Play at least three full normal-speed native loops, without pose/joint guides. View the complete actor and floor. Record the player state before/after each watched interval and any defect times. Do not substitute a contact sheet or hash report for motion viewing.
2. Play the nearest2× normal loop. Then native and2× half-speed loops. Inspect whether the slower view exposes abrupt shell hinging, cloth stretching, leg-length changes, instant recoil or attachment drift that the faster view hides.
3. Play silhouette normal and slower loops. Do both contacts, weight lowering, passing and upward recovery read without ornaments? Is near/far leg separation coherent, or just two shuffling boots under a stiff torso?
4. Examine the eight readable sourceRGBA keys and all adjacent pairs including last→first. Labels are claims to check, not truths. Verify source art is whole-body pose design, not a fixed upper placard with increasingly elaborate leg motion.
5. View raw physical translation separately from any declared shared contact correction. Explain which defects depend on correction. A common root shift can be a legitimate discrete-pixel presentation policy, but cannot rescue a malformed gait or prove anatomical contact.

## Blocking questions

- Is weight supported by the planted leg, with pelvis/chest/head settling and recovering coherently? Does the free leg clearly unload and pass rather than bend sideways in place?
- Are opposite arm/leg actions believable for the actual loadout? A held sword/shield guard may restrict arm swing; it does not justify a motionless torso or dangling grip.
- Do joints retain believable volume and continuous attachment? Look for detached kneecaps, telescoping thighs, thin ribbon shins, hinge gaps and near/far topology changes.
- Is the ground plane coherent? Distinguish perspective depth, camera motion, fixed support and real sliding. Record visible skating or corrective jumps even if a numeric witness passes.
- Do the head, shoulder width, chest, pelvis and boots retain identity/proportions? Are material highlights coherent with shell orientation? Are pixel clusters intentional rather than rotated speckles?
- Does each support phase have readable contact/down/passing/up timing and a smooth continuous wrap? Eight different images are not automatically eight useful key poses.
- Do cape, gear, hands and weapon/shield exhibit appropriate lag, recovery and attachment? Check occlusion throughout the cycle rather than judging the prettiest key.

## Report

Lead with a candid verdict: `blocking_changes`, `useful_experiment_with_limits`, or `ready_for_user_visual_review`. List the strongest observable defect first, with exact pose/time, screenshot or encoded-video timestamp and an actionable whole-body repair. Identify differences from the watched motion reference and failed v20. Separate observed motion from inferred source anatomy.

Do not review toward a requested score, count tests as art points, keep raising numbers merely because iterations exist, or call a small single-view experiment a finished animation library. If a score is requested later, give it after the defect report and explain uncertain/untested categories. User visual acceptance remains separate.

Record playback limitations truthfully: screenshots are selected chronology; they do not prove continuously watched video. Video inspection and PNG inspection each have different coverage. The harness's encoded video has a1ms endpoint packet beyond the exact source-loop interval; don't mistake that packet for an authored extra pose.
