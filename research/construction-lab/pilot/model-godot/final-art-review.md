**Usable as a local prerendered walk benchmark. This is not final user art approval or an original Copper Scout design.** Source manifest: `03fa079e0495dd83f81ad6a00d1778f57e4b7effa885c4d5d54498ebd7c226c8`.

I viewed all 48 source poses, their adjacent and wrap relationships, all 21 chronological actual 2× browser captures spanning three loops, a selected native-size capture, and all eight actual Godot fixed/follow-camera directional screenshots. Captures retain before/after clock ranges; this review does not claim continuously watched video. The JSON binds the exact capture receipt.

The four views now keep consistent hood/body volume and material brightness. The alternating leg phases are convincing in side view; front/back overlap makes the gait subtler but retains direction and anatomy. The carried bow explains the restrained carrying arm, while the free arm participates in the stride. Repeated captured cycles show no obvious large silhouette reset. Twelve held poses still produce a deliberately stepped presentation.

At native size the face remains sparsely described, the muted costume lacks a distinctive authored identity, and thin bow tips/string can merge with legs. These are useful polish targets rather than evidence of a broken directional model. This is modern prerendered sprite imagery with soft alpha, not hand-authored pixel clusters.

Movement is an audition choice, not validated sole contact. The grid makes translation and camera changes visible; it does not demonstrate planted feet. Only Walk is supported. This verdict does not approve shooting, idle/action transitions, a full animation library or final production art.
